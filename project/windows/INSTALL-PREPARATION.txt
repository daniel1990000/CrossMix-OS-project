PREPARE OUR SMART PRO INSTALL FILES ON WINDOWS

You can do the download/preparation now, before the card reader arrives.
This is for the custom RetroPass PSP project on pinned spruceOS v4.5.4.
It does not activate the interface, repair your current card or flash firmware.

1. Extract the setup kit on your laptop. Open SETUP.cmd and choose prepare,
   or double-click PREPARE-INSTALL.cmd.
2. Press Enter to use Documents. Keep at least 15 GiB free on the Windows
   system drive for the base; copying your games later needs additional space.
3. Press Enter to download the official pinned spruce base (about 1.65 GB),
   or enter the path to an already-downloaded spruceV4.5.4.7z. The helper checks
   its exact size and SHA256 before using it. A different version is refused.
4. If 7-Zip is already installed in its usual Program Files folder, the helper
   extracts the verified archive into a new pinned-base folder. Otherwise it
   leaves the verified archive ready. Install 7-Zip from https://www.7-zip.org/
   and rerun preparation with that cached archive when you want extraction.
   No 7-Zip executable is downloaded or installed automatically.
5. When the reader arrives, first use START-HERE.cmd to make the complete
   verified card backup. For optional game staging, supply its TrimUI-date
   folder here (the folder containing backup-status.json and card-files).
   If you have no backup yet, press Enter to skip this step.
6. The helper lists supported systems. Type PSP,PS for those two, or ALL for
   all listed systems. It copies only the selected supported game files to a
   NEW laptop rom-staging folder, checks source hashes against the backup
   manifest and reads back every copied file. Enter alone creates only a plan.

The output stays on your laptop. Do not copy the whole preparation folder onto
an SD card. Pinned-base is the upstream base, not the activated custom OS.
The separately prepared custom candidate still needs your actual device and
recovery review. Production activation stays disabled.

PSP from an old PPSSPP folder maps to PSP; PlayStation remains PS. Paths inside
console folders are preserved, including CUE/BIN tracks and M3U disc lists.
Missing, outside-console, unsupported or unselected CUE/M3U companion files
stop copying. PS audio companions may be preserved without claiming that the
selected emulator can play every format. Nested M3U lists require review. CCD requires its same-name IMG companion.
MDF/TOC/CBN layouts are review-only rather than assuming their dependencies.
If both PSP and PPSSPP folders map to PSP, the helper stops instead of silently
merging them. Unknown systems/extensions are recorded for review, not deleted.

Saves, memory cards, states, BIOS, settings and cover art remain in the complete
backup. They are not automatically mixed into the new base. Their actual paths
and emulator compatibility must be checked before import. The manifest and
local reports may identify your game files; nothing is uploaded automatically.

This helper never selects or writes the microSD and refuses an interactive
workspace outside the Windows system drive or resembling an existing card root.
It creates new output trees; errors retain incomplete output for inspection.
A file-copy check is not a boot or gameplay test. Windows PowerShell 5.1 and
physical device operation remain untested; host fixtures use PowerShell 7.4.6.
